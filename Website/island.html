Good to know.

Which Macs does it work on?

Only MacBooks with a notch: MacBook Pro 14-inch and 16-inch (2021 and later), and MacBook Air 13-inch and 15-inch with M2 or later. It requires macOS 13 or later.

Why create an account?

Your account holds your license, so you can restore it after a reinstall or a new Mac.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

Donut Island reads the notification banners macOS already shows for messages, email and calls. Calendar uses its own permission.

Will macOS still show its own banner?

Yes. Apple doesn’t let apps hide the system banner.

Is it on the Mac App Store?

No. Reading notifications needs Accessibility access, which the App Store doesn’t allow. You download it directly.

Does it show Now Playing?

Not yet. Apple blocked the private interface in macOS 15.4.
