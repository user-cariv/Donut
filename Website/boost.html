How it works

Bring your connections
to the same PC.

Connect the PC to Wi-Fi, then connect phones by USB. Turn on USB tethering on Android, or Personal Hotspot on iPhone and tap “Trust” if prompted. Windows needs Apple Devices or iTunes installed for iPhone USB tethering. Boost detects active network adapters and can split downloads across them when the download server supports byte ranges.

01

Connect

Use home Wi-Fi plus USB-tethered Android phones or iPhones on one Windows PC.

02

Paste a link

Enter a direct file download URL in Donut Boost and choose where to save the file.

03

Download together

Watch per-connection activity and combined download progress in one window.

In the app: choose Combine Both, Home Wi-Fi Only, or Mobile Data Only; set custom download speed limits (or uncapped); toggle individual connections; pause, resume, or cancel a download; and choose its save folder.

Adapters refresh automatically while the app is open. Donut Boost can recover active download chunks if a phone disconnects, and periodically checks tethered mobile connections to help keep them available.

Setup help: Android USB tethering · iPhone Personal Hotspot on Windows. Multi-connection speed depends on the server supporting byte-range downloads. Links without that support may not benefit from combined connections and can fail.
